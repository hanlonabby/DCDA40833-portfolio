7-Eleven Marketing Campaign

Fall 2025

Project Overview

In my Campaigns Capstone course for my Strategic Communication major, I developed a marketing campaign with a group of peers for 7-Eleven called “Own Your Wins.” The goal was to shift Gen Z’s perception of 7-Eleven hot foods by positioning them as the perfect reward for everyday accomplishments, whether that’s finishing an exam, hanging out with friends, or getting through a long shift. I helped shape the creative direction, audience personas, and social media strategy by focusing on Gen Z’s reward culture and their strong connection to platforms like TikTok and Instagram.

Key Highlights

  • Developed the campaign concept “Own Your Wins,” which positioned 7-Eleven hot foods as rewards for everyday milestones.
  • Created target audience personas representing Gen Z lifestyles, including high school students, college students, and young professionals.
  • Designed a social media strategy centered on TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, and X to reach Gen Z where they already spend their time online.
  • Integrated influencer partnerships and user generated content to encourage audiences to share their own “wins” using the campaign hashtag.

Skills Demonstrated

Campaign Strategy, Audience Research & Consumer Insights, Social Media Marketing, Influencer Marketing Strategy, Creative Direction, Brand Positioning, Content Strategy
