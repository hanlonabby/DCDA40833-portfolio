Tecovas Brand Strategy

Fall 2023

Project Overview

For this my Research class in Strategic Communication, my team and I conducted a research report analyzing the western boot brand Tecovas and its communication strategy with younger audiences. We examined the brand’s current positioning, performed a situational and consumer analysis, and conducted qualitative research with Gen Z participants to better understand their perceptions of cowboy boots and social media marketing. Our findings helped us develop strategic recommendations to help Tecovas strengthen its messaging and connect more effectively with Gen Z consumers.

Key Highlights

  • Conducted a situational analysis examining Tecovas’ brand positioning, pricing strategy, and digital presence.
  • Researched Millennial and Gen Z consumer demographics and purchasing behaviors to identify potential audience shifts.
  • Led a qualitative focus group with participants ages 18–24 to explore attitudes toward cowboy boots, social media advertising, and brand perception.
  • Developed strategic recommendations including Instagram Live product launches, university collaborations, and influencer partnerships to increase Gen Z engagement.

Skills Demonstrated

Market Research, Consumer Insights, Qualitative Research (Focus Groups), Brand Analysis, Strategic Recommendations, Audience Analysis, Marketing Strategy, Presentation Design
